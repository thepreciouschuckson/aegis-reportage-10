A learning platform built by [Miva Open University](https://miva.edu.ng) student Esih Egwurube has won a Lagos showcase of artificial intelligence (AI) solutions for education and lifelong learning.

The platform, Coteacher, took first place at the showcase at the Lagos Civic Centre on Wednesday. The event was part of the "Codex in Action: Engineering the Future of Learning with AI" initiative, organised by Andela, OpenAI and Miva Open University.

Coteacher helps teachers turn recorded lessons into personalised learning materials. It creates different versions of a lesson based on each learner's needs and learning history.

With teacher approval, students can download lessons for offline use, get alternative explanations and activities when they struggle, and resume lessons where they stopped. When online, students can use an AI-powered voice tutor, and their progress syncs with their teachers.

The showcase brought together 100 developers from Miva and 100 senior engineers from Andela. The participants trained in OpenAI Codex, then worked together for three weeks to build practical AI applications.

Egwurube said the training gave him technical knowledge and practical experience he can apply to real-world problems. He said the programme helped him improve his workflow and build solutions with measurable impact.

The organisers said the wider group of 200 developers improved their assessed proficiency by 36 percentage points over their baseline after two weeks of applied learning, [according to the event press release](https://africa.com/ama/andela-openai-and-miva-open-university-bring-200-engineers-together-to-build-ai-powered-learning-solutions).

The initiative is part of the Codex Champions Series, a pan-African programme that shows how developers can use AI coding tools to build practical solutions. Lagos hosted the second chapter after Nairobi. Cape Town is scheduled to host the next event on November 15.
