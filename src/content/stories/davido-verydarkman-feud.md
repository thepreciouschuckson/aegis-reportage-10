The disagreement between Afrobeats star David Adeleke, known as Davido, and social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, known as VeryDarkMan, has drawn wide reaction online. Fresh allegations about how their friendship ended set off the latest round.

The row intensified on Thursday, November 5, 2026, when VeryDarkMan released a video [explaining his fallout with the musician](https://leadership.ng/why-my-friendship-with-davido-ended-verydarkman/). He said the friendship began when Davido followed him on social media while he campaigned for justice over the death of singer Mohbad.

VeryDarkMan said he first admired Davido, then grew friendly with him. He sometimes promoted the singer's music and supported his activities online.

He alleged the relationship soured after he found a WhatsApp group conversation that reportedly involved people from Davido's music circle and other entertainment figures. He claimed some participants discussed plans to have him arrested over his role in the Mohbad campaign.

VeryDarkMan said he tried several times to contact Davido to seek clarification about the alleged involvement of people close to the singer. He said he got no response.

He described the episode as a betrayal and said he expected an explanation from someone he considered a friend. The allegations have not been independently established. The reports reviewed did not include a detailed response from Davido to the latest claims.

Earlier, reports said the two had unfollowed each other on Instagram after content creator Kokopee [failed to reconcile them](https://www.legit.ng/entertainment/music/1738972-davido-takes-action-verydarkman-instagram-ongoing-feud-obo/). Kokopee said publicly that he tried to settle the dispute and that VeryDarkMan felt betrayed by Davido. Davido then replied with a cap emoji, which many social media users read as a rejection of that account. A video also circulated of Davido singing along to lyrics from Zlatan Ibile's song "Inferno", which some listeners read as aimed at VeryDarkMan.

VeryDarkMan also rejected online claims that Davido had given him financial help or helped build his public profile. He asked anyone making those claims to show evidence and said the claims about financial support are false.

Social media users have reacted in different ways. Some support Davido, while others defend VeryDarkMan's decision to speak publicly.

The row shows how quickly disputes between public figures can become major online talking points when social platforms amplify the claims and reactions.
