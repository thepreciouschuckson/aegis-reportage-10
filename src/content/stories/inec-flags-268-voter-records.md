The [Independent National Electoral Commission](https://inecnigeria.org) (INEC) has flagged 268 records on Nigeria's preliminary voters register over suspected AI-generated images and other irregularities. The flag comes ahead of the 2027 general elections, [Punch reported](https://punchng.com/inec-flags-268-records-over-ai-photos-irregularities).

The commission's Automated Biometric Identification System (ABIS) detected the records. INEC is now investigating whether they contain fake images or other irregularities, said Lawrence Bayode, the commission's Director of Information and Communication Technology.

INEC said not every AI-edited image will be removed. Genuine photos improved with AI features on mobile phones remain valid. The commission will remove only images found to be entirely AI-generated.

The finding has raised concern among Nigerians about the credibility of the register.

INEC must keep the voters register accurate and credible as the 2027 elections approach. INEC must also show that genuine voters will not be affected.
