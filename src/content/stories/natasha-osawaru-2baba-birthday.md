Edo lawmaker Natasha Osawaru marked her birthday on Saturday, November 14, and social media users quickly linked it to a post from her estranged husband, Afrobeats veteran 2Baba.

On Friday, November 13, 2Baba posted a photo of his ex-wife, Annie Idibia, on his Instagram story with a short birthday wish. [Legit.ng reported the post](https://www.legit.ng/entertainment/celebrities/1741245-2baba-sends-sweet-bday-message-estranged-wife-annie-idibia-love-strong-pass-super-glue/) the same day.

Osawaru shared her own birthday message on Instagram the next day. She thanked God and said the past year had been rough but had turned into a blessing. [Her post](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dfs_NUCgq4O/) drew a wave of comments.

Many users noticed that her birthday falls close to Annie Idibia's. Some made jokes about it. Others asked whether the timing meant something. One user pointed out that Osawaru had not celebrated on this date before and asked what had changed. Another said second wives were not being favoured this year.

2Baba ended his 13-year marriage to Annie Idibia before he married Osawaru in 2025, according to [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/entertainment/celebrities/1741303-natasha-osawaru-marks-birthday-hours-2baba-celebrates-wife-annie-macaulay-nigerians-react/). His separation from Osawaru has since kept both relationships in public view.

Legit.ng's report did not include any comment from 2Baba or Osawaru on the timing of the posts. Aegis Reportage could not independently confirm any link between them. The reactions above come from public comments on social media and do not show what either person intended.
